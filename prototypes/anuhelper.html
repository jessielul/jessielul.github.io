Add your courses in ANUHub

NavBar›Menu›ANUHub›Enrolment›Enrolment
1Click Enrolment Details for your session
2Click Add, then enter the class number
3Repeat for each course, then Continue
4Enter a permission number if asked
5Click Save. Your courses aren’t added until you do!
Class numbers are on each course's Class tab in Programs and Courses.

Your ANU password

Pick what’s happening and follow the steps.
1Find the temporary password ANU emailed you
2Sign in at myaccount.microsoft.com with your ANU email
3You’ll be asked to change your password straight away
4Set up MFA with the Microsoft Authenticator app
1Go to passwordreset.microsoftonline.com
2Enter u1234567@anu.edu.au (your ID) and the CAPTCHA
3Approve the request in Authenticator, or enter its code
4Enter your new password twice, then click Finish
You can only do this once in 24 hours.
1Sign in at myaccount.microsoft.com
2Click Change Password in the menu
3Approve the MFA prompt, enter your new password and Submit
Still locked out? ANU IT Service Desk +61 2 6125 4321 · Mon to Fri, 8am to 5pm · servicedesk@anu.edu.au. You need to call yourself.

In ANUHub, choose Drop or Swap next to the course.
NavBar›Menu›ANUHub›Enrolment›Enrolment

Last day to add courses3 Aug
Census date31 Aug

Dropping may carry academic or financial penalties, depending on when you drop compared with the census date.
International students: going below 24 units needs a Reduced Study Load Application because of visa rules.

Pay my fees

What happens after you enrol, and where to go next.
1Your course fees show in ANUHub straight after you enrol
2Your invoice arrives by ANU student email within the first two weeks of your session
3Pay, set up HELP deferral or get invoice help on the Fees & Payments page
No invoice yet? Check your ANU student email, not your personal inbox.
